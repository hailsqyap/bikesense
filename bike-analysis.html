Analysis settings (thresholds)

Every threshold here is a choice you should justify and test. Course checklist question 3 asks whether a reasonable parameter change would change your answer, so try a few values and record what happens. The settings you used are saved in the export.

Data quality & cross-checks

Independent checks: GPS speed changes vs. accelerometer, GPS heading vs. gyroscope, GPS stops vs. vibration stops. Correlations near +1 mean the two sensors agree.

Sound level

Sound features (crowd vs. music vs. wind)

Speech-band ratio and 2–8 Hz modulation high → voices. Rhythm high with low flatness → music with a beat. Low-frequency ratio high → wind or engines.

Stopping & starting (forward acceleration)

Accelerometer forward acceleration (smoothed) vs. acceleration worked out from GPS speed. Grey bands are stops; red triangles are hard braking; green are hard starts.

Turns (gyroscope yaw rate & sideways acceleration)

Positive = turning right. Blue diamonds are detected turns.

Vertical vibration (road roughness, accelerometer-only stop detection)

Phone Z is mostly up/down. Its vibration shows road roughness, and when it drops below the threshold the bike is probably stopped. This is a GPS-free baseline.

Route segments

Busyness score = weighted mix of normalised noise, % voices-like seconds, stops per minute and hard brakes per minute. The weights are in the settings. Music-like sound is reported but not counted as crowding.
